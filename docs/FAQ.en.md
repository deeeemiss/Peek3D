# Peek3D — License FAQ

*[Italiano](FAQ.it.md)*

Practical answers about buying, activating, and troubleshooting a Peek3D
license. For what personal data this involves, see the
[privacy notice](PRIVACY.en.md).

## Trial

### How does the free trial work?

You can open **10 distinct files** for free. Opening a file you've already
opened before doesn't use up a slot — only genuinely new files count. Once
you've opened 10 distinct files, opening any *further new* file is blocked
until you activate a license. Files you already opened during the trial
stay reachable forever, license or not.

### I bought a license — do I lose access to files I opened during the trial?

No. Activating a license removes the block entirely; every file you could
already open, you can still open.

## Buying

### What does €19.99 actually get me?

A perpetual license for **one Mac**, with **all future Peek3D updates
included at no extra cost**, forever. There's no subscription, no renewal,
and no expiry date on the license itself.

### I use Peek3D on more than one Mac. Do I need to buy it twice?

You need one activation per Mac. Rather than buying two full-price
licenses, buy a **2-license or 3-license pack** — it's discounted compared
to buying single licenses separately, and each key in the pack activates
one Mac.

### Can I use one license on two Macs?

No — **one license = one Mac**, verified online. If you try to activate a
key that's already occupying its Mac seat(s), activation will fail until
you free one up (see "I got a new Mac" below) or buy an additional license.
If you regularly use two Macs, a 2-license pack is the intended way to do
it and avoids running into this at all.

## Activation

### How do I enter my license key?

Open **Settings ▸ Cambia licenza…** (or, if you're still on the trial
screen, the "Ho già una licenza" / "I already have a license" link) and
paste in the key from your purchase email. The key is checked
cryptographically **offline** — you don't need an internet connection for
this step to work.

### What happens after activation — does Peek3D need to stay online?

No. After activation, Peek3D periodically re-verifies your license against
our activation service in the background. If it can't reach that service —
no internet, a flaky connection, the service being briefly down — **the app
keeps working normally for up to 30 days** since the last successful check.
It's not a hard, immediate requirement; it's a grace period built in
specifically so a normal offline stretch (a flight, a trip, a bad week of
Wi-Fi) never locks you out.

### What happens if I go offline for longer than 30 days?

Once the grace period runs out without a successful check-in, Peek3D asks
you to reconnect to re-verify. Reconnecting for a few seconds — any
internet connection, even briefly — restores normal operation. Nothing is
lost: your license and your files are untouched, the app just needs to
confirm the license is still valid.

## Something went wrong

### I lost my license key. How do I get it back?

Use **"Recover license"** (in the app or on the purchase site) and enter
the email address you purchased with. If we find a paid order for that
email, we'll email your key again. For your privacy, this always shows the
same generic confirmation message regardless of whether we actually found
an order — check your inbox (and spam folder) either way.

### I got a new Mac. How do I move my license over?

Before you activate on the new Mac, **free up the seat on the old one**
yourself — from the license management screen in Peek3D, or the equivalent
page linked from your purchase email. Once the seat is free, activate the
same key on the new Mac.

You also get a small number of **free reactivations** built into every
license, specifically to cover normal hardware changes — a new Mac, a
replaced drive, a reinstalled macOS — without needing to contact anyone.

### I replaced the logic board and now I can't activate — what do I do?

This is exactly the case the free-reactivation allowance exists for: a
logic board swap changes the device's hardware identity, so the app will
usually just ask you to reactivate, and one of your included reactivations
covers it automatically.

If the old Mac is genuinely gone (dead, wiped, sold) and you couldn't free
up its seat first, and activation still refuses because the seat is
considered occupied, contact **licenze@peek3d.app** with your order
confirmation — we can release the old seat manually from our side.

### I bought two licenses by accident. Can I get one refunded?

Yes. Email **licenze@peek3d.app** with both order confirmations (or the two
receipt emails) within 14 days of the purchase you want refunded, and we'll
process a refund for the duplicate.

## Refunds

Peek3D comes with a **14-day, no-questions-asked refund**. If you're not
happy with your purchase, email **licenze@peek3d.app** with your order
confirmation within 14 days of buying, and we'll refund it. Refunds are
processed through Polar.sh, the payment provider that handled your
purchase, and typically land back on your original payment method within a
few business days.

## Your license key and your privacy

Your license key contains **your purchase email in plain text** (that's a
deliberate design choice — it's what lets Peek3D show whose license is
active without contacting a server every time). Treat your key like you
would an email address you don't want spammed: it's fine to keep in your
password manager or inbox, but **don't paste it into a public forum, a
public GitHub issue, or a screenshot you're sharing online.**

For the full picture of what data is stored during activation and why, see
the [privacy notice](PRIVACY.en.md).
